Welcome,

posted: 2024/06/08
last edited: 2026/10/22

Originally, this site was created as supplemental practice for The Odin Project curriculum, and while it will still serve this purpose I have also decided that there is no reason that I cannot use it for sharing various information. As such, this site is a bit of a catch-all for anything I wanted to share over the internet.

As many of you may be familiar with, there's always that one go-to family member that people go to for basic tech support. For my family that person is typically me, and as such I plan to make informational posts on resolving issues, and setting up software, with the hope that I can re-use these articles if someone else has the same issue in the future. Having them online has a couple of benefits; it makes them available from anywhere, and it also allows others to share links to these articles with their family members.

With all the being said, as of current, the most common thing you will find on here are guides and bookmark collections for various games. Previously, I had them shared on Discord servers for friends, but since I have qualms with the direction of Discord I thought I would also post them somewhere else so that they are still available in the event I abandon the platform.

Thanks for visiting. I hope that you find something that's useful or informative.
